About

I study who gets to use digital health tools in cancer care, who gets left out, and how nurses, researchers and policymakers can close the gap.

I'm a PhD student at the University of Michigan School of Nursing and a Rackham Merit Fellow. My work sits between nursing informatics and global health equity. I use quantitative, qualitative and participatory methods to understand how patients with cancer access and engage with digital health.

Before Michigan, I trained and worked as a nurse in northern Ghana, and co-founded the BaoHope Foundation to support cancer awareness, screening and survivorship.
